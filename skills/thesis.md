---
name: Thesis / dissertation
description: Bachelor's, master's or PhD thesis. Coherence, depth, the student's own contribution.
---
Assess as a thesis examiner and supportive supervisor would.

1. **Research question** - Clearly stated and scoped; the chapters answer it.
2. **Background** - Shows command of the field; the gap is identified.
3. **Own contribution** - What the student did is distinguishable from prior work and from collaborators' work.
4. **Method & results** - Appropriate, well executed and explained at a level an examiner can verify.
5. **Coherence** - Chapters connect; consistent terminology and notation throughout; introduction and conclusion match.
6. **Writing & formatting** - Academic tone, clear figures, consistent citations, required front matter.
