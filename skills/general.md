---
name: General research paper
description: Any field. Clarity, contribution, evidence and structure.
---
Assess the paper as a careful senior colleague would before submission.

1. **Contribution** - Is the problem and why it matters clear in the first page? Are the contributions stated explicitly and are they actually delivered later?
2. **Positioning** - Does related work show how this differs from the closest prior work, rather than just listing papers?
3. **Method** - Could a competent reader reproduce or check the approach from what's written? Are assumptions stated?
4. **Evidence** - Do the results, proofs or analyses support each claim made in the abstract and introduction? Flag claims stronger than the evidence.
5. **Clarity** - Is the writing precise and easy to follow? Are terms defined before use, figures/tables referenced and readable?
6. **Structure & completeness** - Logical flow, limitations discussed, conclusion matches the body, references complete and consistent.
