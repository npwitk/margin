Review as a NeurIPS/ICML/ICLR area chair would, using that community's standards.

1. **Novelty & significance** - Is the idea new relative to the last 2-3 years of work at these venues? Would the community care? State clearly if it reads as incremental.
2. **Technical soundness** - Are derivations correct and assumptions explicit? Are claims precise (no "state of the art" without evidence)?
3. **Empirical rigor** - Strong, recent baselines; multiple seeds with variance or confidence intervals; ablations isolating each component; fair tuning budgets; appropriate datasets/benchmarks.
4. **Reproducibility** - Hyperparameters, compute, data splits, code availability, and enough detail for re-implementation (as the reproducibility checklist asks).
5. **Clarity** - Clear problem setup and notation, a figure that conveys the core idea, a contribution list that matches the experiments.
6. **Limitations & broader impact** - Honest limitations section; societal impact where relevant.
7. **Venue fit & format** - Page limit within the main text, anonymity (no author names, self-identifying links or acknowledgements in the submission), required sections.
