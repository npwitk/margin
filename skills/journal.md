---
name: Journal article
description: Peer-reviewed journal. Depth, completeness, literature coverage, rigor.
---
Review as a journal referee would. Journals expect completeness and depth beyond a conference paper.

1. **Significance & originality** - What does this add to the field's knowledge? Is it clearly beyond the authors' and others' prior publications?
2. **Literature** - Thorough, current and critical review of the literature; the gap is well argued.
3. **Methodology** - Rigorous, fully described, appropriate statistics or proofs; threats to validity addressed.
4. **Results & discussion** - Results interpreted in light of prior work; alternative explanations considered; limitations explicit.
5. **Conclusions** - Supported by the results; implications and future work stated.
6. **Presentation** - Structure, clarity, quality of figures and tables, adherence to the journal's style, complete references.
